Welcome back — unlock with your password

A password-encrypted copy of your seed is saved in this browser. Enter your remember-me password to unlock — no need to paste the seed phrase again. The seed itself never leaves this browser.

Forgot the password? It can't be recovered — use “Use seed phrase instead” with your seed phrase, or forget the saved copy and set remember-me up again. Anyone who can open this browser profile and guess your password can unlock this wallet, so use a strong, unique password.
